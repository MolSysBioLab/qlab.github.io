What we study

From molecular mechanisms to metabolic phenotypes

The Qadir Lab investigates how endocrine-cell function is regulated across biological sex and metabolic context. We connect molecular mechanisms with islet physiology and whole-body outcomes to identify pathways that preserve β-cell health.

β

β-cell biology

Insulin secretion, calcium signaling, cellular stress, and mechanisms that preserve β-cell identity and functional capacity.

♀

Sex differences

Sex chromosomes, gonadal hormones, and estrogen receptor pathways that influence endocrine adaptation and diabetes susceptibility.

◌

Systems metabolism

Transcriptomic, spatial, imaging, and physiological approaches that connect cell states to organism-level metabolic outcomes.

Research approach

Mechanism-driven and multidisciplinary

We combine experimentally rigorous in vivo and ex vivo systems with quantitative data analysis to identify mechanisms of endocrine resilience and dysfunction.

  • Mouse model development and metabolic phenotyping
  • Glucose-, hormone-, and nutrient-stimulated insulin secretion assays
  • Islet isolation, confocal microscopy, and live-cell calcium imaging
  • Bulk, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomic analysis
  • Computational systems biology and reproducible statistical workflows
