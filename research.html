Research

Understanding endocrine resilience across biological sex and metabolic context.

Our work integrates experimental physiology, molecular biology, quantitative imaging, and computational systems biology.

β

β-cell function and failure

We investigate glucose sensing, stimulus-secretion coupling, calcium dynamics, stress adaptation, and pathways that preserve β-cell identity.

♀

Sex-specific endocrinology

We examine how sex chromosomes, reproductive hormones, and estrogen receptors shape islet biology and diabetes susceptibility.

◌

Metabolic systems biology

We integrate transcriptomics, spatial methods, and computational modeling to link cell-state changes with physiological outcomes.

Methods

Experimental and computational toolkit

  • Mouse model development and metabolic phenotyping
  • GTT, ITT, GSIS, and hormone-stimulated secretion assays
  • Islet isolation, confocal microscopy, and calcium imaging
  • Bulk, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomic analysis
  • Statistical learning and reproducible computational workflows
